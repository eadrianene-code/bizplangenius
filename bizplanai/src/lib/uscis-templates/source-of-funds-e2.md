## 2.4 At-risk and irrevocable commitment

The {{investment_amount}} is fully at risk of loss in the event the enterprise fails. Specifically:

- No portion of the funds is held in escrow, held by a third-party administrator, or otherwise reversible upon visa denial.
- A substantial portion of the funds is committed to fixed-asset purchases, lease obligations, and pre-launch operating expenses that have no resale value at par.
- The remaining capital is held in {{business_name}}'s operating account and is subject to draw-down per the deployment schedule in the financial model.
