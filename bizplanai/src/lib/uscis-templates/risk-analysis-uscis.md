## 10.2 RFE (Request for Evidence) preparedness

If the adjudicator issues an RFE, the firm of record is positioned to respond to the most common RFE topics for {{visa_category}} cases:

- **Source of funds documentation expansion:** Firm of record retains direct contact with the petitioner's banks in {{investor_country}} and can obtain additional bank records, tax records, and asset-sale documentation within standard timelines.
- **Marginality re-analysis:** Firm of record can produce updated financial projections incorporating any new operating data available at RFE timing.
- **Real-and-active operations evidence:** Firm of record can produce updated photos of premises, equipment receipts, vendor invoices, and customer evidence as of the RFE date.
- **Petitioner credentials:** Firm of record can produce additional employment verification letters, academic transcripts, and prior business records.
