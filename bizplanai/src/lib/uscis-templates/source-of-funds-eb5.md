## 2.3 Documentation standards (EB-5-specific)

For each source listed above, the firm of record provides the following EB-5-grade documentation:

**For accumulated savings from employment income:**

- Tax returns from {{investor_country}} for the relevant accumulation years
- Employment verification letters with title, dates, salary
- Bank statements showing accumulation pattern
- Payroll records or equivalent

**For sale of personal or business assets:**

- Sale agreement (translated where applicable)
- Title transfer or share register entry
- Deposit records showing receipt of sale proceeds
- Tax filings reflecting capital gains
- Source-of-asset documentation (how the asset itself was lawfully acquired)

**For inheritance or gift:**

- Death certificate or gift letter
- Will or probate documentation
- Donor source-of-funds documentation (bank statements, tax records, employment history) demonstrating that the donor acquired the funds lawfully
- Bank records of the inheritance or gift transfer

**For loans secured by petitioner's assets (where allowed):**

- Loan agreement with arm's-length terms
- Collateral documentation
- Demonstration that the collateral itself was lawfully acquired
- Bank records of loan disbursement

## 2.4 Path of funds

Capital traveled from origin accounts to {{business_name}}'s U.S. operating account through the following path:

1. **Origin (in {{investor_country}}):** Funds originated in the petitioner's documented accounts as detailed in subsection 2.2 above. Each origin account is supported by documentation per subsection 2.3.
2. **Aggregation:** Funds were consolidated into the petitioner's primary {{investor_country}} bank account in preparation for the international transfer. Consolidation records are provided in firm exhibits.
3. **Currency conversion and international wire:** Funds were converted from {{investor_country_currency}} to USD and wired to the petitioner's U.S. personal account at {{us_bank_or_placeholder}}. Wire confirmation, exchange-rate documentation, and any intermediary bank records are provided in firm exhibits.
4. **Operating account transfer:** From the petitioner's U.S. personal account, funds were transferred to {{business_name}}'s U.S. operating account, completing the at-risk deployment. Operating account statements are provided in firm exhibits.

## 2.6 At-risk and irrevocable commitment

The {{investment_amount}} is fully at risk of loss in the event the enterprise fails. Specifically:

- No portion of the funds is held in escrow or otherwise reversible upon I-526E denial
- A substantial portion of the funds is committed to fixed-asset purchases, lease obligations, and pre-launch operating expenses with no resale value at par
- The remaining capital is held in {{business_name}}'s operating account subject to draw-down per the deployment schedule

The at-risk and irrevocable nature of the investment satisfies the standard set forth in Matter of Izummi and reaffirmed by the EB-5 Reform and Integrity Act of 2022.
