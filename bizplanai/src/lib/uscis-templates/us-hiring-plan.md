## 8.4 Hiring criteria and recruitment

All U.S. hires will be made through standard U.S. labor market channels (job boards, professional recruiters, direct outreach). The petitioner does not intend to fill any position with a foreign national requiring sponsorship beyond the petitioner's own {{visa_category}} status.

Compensation ranges reflect BLS published data for the {{us_location}} area for the corresponding Standard Occupational Classification (SOC) codes. Where the role does not map cleanly to a published SOC, the range reflects the petitioner's market research and adviser input on competitive wages for similar roles in the local market.

## 8.6 Indirect and induced economic impact

In addition to the {{cum_hires_y5}} direct jobs created by Year 5, {{business_name}} will generate indirect economic impact through:

- Vendor and supplier purchases (detailed in Section 9, Operations Plan)
- Local service spending by U.S.-based employees
- Tax revenue (federal, state of {{us_state}}, and local)

While indirect and induced job multipliers (such as the RIMS II model) are typically applied in EB-5 contexts, for the {{visa_category}} non-marginality analysis the direct-hire count and payroll above are sufficient to demonstrate that the enterprise materially exceeds a marginal operation under 8 CFR 214.2(e)(15).
